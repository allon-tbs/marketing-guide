Customer Lifecycle Stage Definitions

Tembusu Financial Services · SleekFlow CRM Standard Operating Procedure · April 2026

↗

Progressing stages

Stages for contacts that are currently engaging or moving forward in your customer lifecycle.

⠿
New Lead DEFAULT
A contact has entered the CRM for the first time — via Meta ad, website enquiry, live chat, or WhatsApp inbound. No meaningful interaction has taken place yet. Automated welcome message may have been sent. Read more Read less
⠿
Contacted
The team has made first contact and received a reply. A two-way conversation has been initiated but the lead has not yet been assessed or qualified. Read more Read less
⠿
Qualified
The lead has shared sufficient information (loan type, amount, purpose, collateral) to determine they are a potential fit for Tembusu's products or a referral partner. A sales call has been proposed. Read more Read less
⠿
Sales Call
A call has been scheduled or completed with the loan advisor. The team is actively assessing the lead's requirements and presenting suitable financing options. Read more Read less
⠿
Obtain Docs
The lead has indicated intent to proceed. The team is collecting required documents (NRIC, NOA, payslips, CBS, property details, etc.) for formal loan assessment or referral processing. Read more Read less
⠿
Refer Out
The lead does not qualify for Tembusu's own products but has consented to referral to a financing partner. Documents are being or have been forwarded to the relevant partner. Tembusu earns a referral commission on successful cases. Read more Read less
⠿
Build Case
The lead qualifies for a Tembusu loan and the team is actively structuring the offer. This includes conducting property valuation, finalising terms, and preparing the offer letter. Read more Read less
⠿
Partner Engaged
A financing partner has been formally engaged on a referred case and is actively reviewing or processing the lead's application. Tembusu is monitoring progress pending the partner's decision. Read more Read less
⠿
Credit Comm
The loan case has been submitted to Tembusu's internal credit committee for final review and approval. All documents have been collected and the case has been assessed and endorsed for committee sign-off. Applicable to Tembusu direct loans only. Read more Read less
⠿
TBS Won
Tembusu's credit committee has approved the loan and the offer letter has been accepted by the borrower. The loan has been successfully disbursed. Case is closed as won for Tembusu. Read more Read less
⠿
Partner Won
A referred case has been successfully approved and disbursed by the financing partner. Tembusu's referral commission is due or has been received. Case is closed as won via partner. Read more Read less
↘

Lost stages

Stages for contacts that are no longer progressing in your customer lifecycle, such as when a conversation, deal, or case has ended.

⠿
Low Value (<$10K)
Lead is seeking a loan amount below Tembusu's minimum threshold. Declined at first contact with a polite rejection message. No referral made. Read more Read less
⠿
MIA
Lead became unresponsive after initial contact or during qualification. Re-engagement sequences (Day 7, Day 14) have been exhausted with no reply. Read more Read less
⠿
Lost – Too Exp
Lead was presented with loan terms but declined to proceed due to interest rates or fees being outside their acceptable range. No further negotiation was possible within Tembusu's approved parameters. Read more Read less
⠿
Lost – Other Funders
Lead chose to proceed with another lender or financing institution instead of Tembusu. Case is closed with no further action required. Read more Read less
⠿
TBS Reject
Lead does not meet Tembusu's internal lending criteria. Reasons may include insufficient collateral, unsupported property type (e.g. JTC leasehold), overseas applicant, or profile assessed as too high-risk. Read more Read less
⠿
Partner Reject
Lead was referred to a financing partner but did not meet the partner's qualifying criteria. No further referral options are available. Case is closed with no commission earned. Read more Read less
