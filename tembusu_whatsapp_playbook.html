Core Engagement Principles

The fundamentals that govern every customer interaction
⚡

Call First, Qualify Second

Get to the call offer within 3 messages maximum. Every extra question before offering the call is a drop-off risk. Use the call to collect detailed information — not WhatsApp.

🎯

Two Options, Not Open Questions

"Are you free this morning or after lunch?" converts far better than "When are you free?" Force a choice. Specificity signals professionalism and gets faster responses.

🤝

Use the Advisor's Name

"I'll connect you with Shirlyn, our loan advisor" makes the handoff feel personal and warm — not a cold transfer to a call centre. Always name the person.

⏰

3–6 Hour Call Target

All new Meta leads should have a call offered and scheduled within 3–6 hours of first contact. Speed is itself a differentiator from banks.

💬

Watch the 24h Window

Once a lead replies, you have 24 hours to send free-form messages. After that, you need a pre-approved HSM template to reopen the conversation. Never let hot leads go cold.

⚖️

Never Promise, Always Assess

Do not guarantee approval, quote specific rates, or make superlative claims in chat. Use "assess", "explore", "indicative" — always. MinLaw compliance is non-negotiable.

FAQ Response Library

Ready-to-paste answers for common lead questions
🏦 General — About Tembusu
Is Tembusu a licensed moneylender?
Yes — Tembusu Financial Services is a licensed moneylender regulated by the Ministry of Law (MinLaw) under the Moneylenders Act. We are not a broker. You deal directly with us from enquiry through to disbursement, with no third-party referrals.
Are there broker fees or hidden charges?
No. We are a direct lender — no broker fees, no middlemen, no hidden charges. You'll only be charged once your loan is approved, and all fees are disclosed clearly in your offer letter before you sign anything.
How is Tembusu different from a bank?
Banks operate under strict MAS regulations including TDSR, rigid credit scoring, and committee-based approvals. We use proprietary capital and our own underwriting model — assessing each borrower's full context rather than just a credit score. This means faster decisions (same-day), more flexibility on eligibility, and no TDSR ceiling on most of our products.
Who are the people behind Tembusu?
Tembusu was founded by the ex-founders of sgCarMart — Singapore's #1 car marketplace. The leadership team brings over 50 years of combined experience across automotive financing, private lending, and property finance. Every deal is reviewed by senior team members — not a junior committee.
💰 Rates, Amounts & Process
What interest rates do you charge?
Interest rates vary based on loan type, amount, collateral, and your profile. We're not able to quote a rate before assessing your situation — but our rates are competitive and fully disclosed in the offer letter. There are no surprises after disbursement. The best next step is a quick call with our advisor to get an indicative assessment.
⚖️ Do not quote specific rates in chat — MinLaw compliance
How much can I borrow?
Our loans range from $10,000 to $50 million depending on loan type and collateral. Property-backed loans can go from $100K to $20M in-house. Business loans are assessed case by case. Tell us your requirement and we'll let you know what's realistic for your situation.
How fast can I get the money?
Once your loan is approved, funds can be disbursed within 24 hours. Our assessment is typically completed on the same business day, provided all required documents are submitted. This is significantly faster than the 2–4 week timeline typical of banks.
What documents do I need?
This depends on your loan type. As a general guide: NRIC/passport, proof of income or business financials (NOA/statements), and documents related to the collateral (e.g. property title deed, car ownership). Our advisor will give you a precise list after the initial assessment call — we don't make you prepare a stack of documents before we've even had a conversation.
🏠 Property-Backed Loans
What is a bridging loan and how does it work?
A bridging loan is short-term financing that covers the gap between two property transactions — typically when you need to complete a purchase before your current property has sold. Unlike banks, we don't require your existing sale to be confirmed first. If you own property and have a clear exit strategy, we can typically structure a solution quickly. Minimum: $100K. Tenure: up to 12 months.
What is a caveat loan?
A caveat loan lets you access cash by lodging a caveat against your property title as security. It's a property-backed loan that gives you liquidity without selling the property. Suitable for property owners who need funds quickly and have equity in their property. We can typically assess same-day if documents are in order.
Can I get a loan against my HDB flat?
HDB properties cannot be mortgaged by private lenders for most standard loan products. However, depending on your situation and what the funds are for, there may be alternative structures available. Speak to our advisor directly — don't rule it out before we've assessed it.
Can I get a loan against a JTC property?
Unfortunately no. JTC's lease conditions restrict mortgaging of JTC leasehold properties to MAS-licensed banks only. Private lenders like Tembusu are not permitted to take JTC properties as collateral. We'd recommend approaching DBS, OCBC, or UOB for JTC industrial property financing.
Does TDSR apply to your property loans?
TDSR does not apply to most of our property-backed loan products. This is one of our key advantages over banks — borrowers who are blocked by TDSR at a bank can often still qualify with us. Our advisor can confirm TDSR applicability for your specific situation on the call.
🚗 Automotive Loans
Can I get a car loan if I'm self-employed?
Yes — this is something we help with regularly. We accept varied income sources including commission, rental income, and business income. We assess your financial situation holistically, not just payslips. Many of our car loan clients are self-employed, freelancers, or small business owners.
Can I get a car loan as a foreigner / expat?
Yes, we do assist foreigners and PRs with car financing. Eligibility depends on your employment pass type, income, and down payment. Speak to our advisor — we assess expat applications on a case-by-case basis and can often find a solution where banks can't.
Can I use my fully paid car to get a cash loan?
Yes — this is called a vehicle refinancing or vehicle equity loan. If you own a car outright (including PHV/Grab vehicles), you can borrow against it as collateral. The loan amount depends on the vehicle's current market value. Share your car details and how much you need and we'll assess quickly.
Do you offer COE renewal loans?
Yes. We offer COE renewal loans that cover the full COE cost so you're not scrambling for cash when renewal comes around. We recommend planning ahead — contact us at least 1–2 months before your COE expiry for a smooth process.
🏢 Business & SME Loans
How long does my business need to be operating?
We work with businesses from as early as 3 months of operation for most products. Early-stage businesses may qualify through alternative structures, especially if there are existing assets. Don't assume you won't qualify — have the conversation first.
What if my business has been rejected by a bank?
Bank rejections are often based on rigid criteria — TDSR limits, minimum revenue thresholds, credit scoring models. We use proprietary underwriting and assess each business on its own merits. Many of our clients come to us after bank rejections and find a workable solution. Share your situation and we'll be straight with you about what's possible.
Do I need collateral for a business loan?
Not necessarily. We offer both secured (property-backed) and unsecured business loans. Unsecured loans are assessed based on your business's creditworthiness and financial history. Property-backed loans allow access to larger amounts. Our advisor can recommend the right structure based on your profile.
