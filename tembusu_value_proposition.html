01 — Market Context

The Problem We Solve

Singapore's lending market is bifurcated. Banks come with rigid criteria — TDSR limits, income documentation requirements, minimum operating periods, and credit-score gatekeeping. On the other end, unregulated lenders prey on the desperate. The result: a vast underserved middle ground of creditworthy businesses and individuals who simply don't fit a bank's template.

Who Gets Left Behind

🏢

SMEs & Startups

Under 2 years in operation, irregular cash flow, or in sectors banks classify as high-risk. Banks demand 2–3 years of audited accounts most young businesses can't produce.

🏠

Property Borrowers Hit by TDSR

Strong assets, clear exit strategy — but a debt-servicing ratio above the bank's 55% ceiling. Bridge financing, caveat loans, and private property mortgages blocked.

🚗

Car Buyers & COE Renewers

Self-employed, foreign workers, or borrowers with imperfect credit declined for bank HP loans — or facing COE renewal deadlines banks won't rush to meet.

What They Actually Need

"I don't need charity. I need a lender who actually looks at my business, not just my credit score — and can move fast enough to matter."

Speed, flexibility, and a human decision-making process are the three things rejected borrowers consistently seek — and fail to find at a bank.

When a supplier demands payment in 48 hours, a COE expires next week, or a commercial property opportunity has a one-week exclusive window, waiting three weeks for a bank credit committee is not an option.

Tembusu was built precisely for these moments.

03 — Segment Value Propositions

Tailored Promises for Each Audience

Tembusu's master VP adapts across five core audience segments. Each carries the same DNA — speed, flexibility, transparency — expressed in language that speaks directly to that segment's specific pressure points.

🏢
SMEs & Business Owners
Working capital · Expansion · Trade financing · Caveat loans

Their pain: Bank rejected them. Not because the business is failing — because it's too young, too seasonal, or too complex for a bank's credit model. Meanwhile, a supplier is calling, a contract is at risk, or a commercial opportunity has a closing deadline.

"Your business doesn't have to fit the bank's mould to get funded. Tembusu looks at where your business is going — not just where it's been. Working capital from $100K to $20M, approved in hours, in your account in 24."
🏠
Property Owners & Investors
Bridging loans · Caveat loans · Commercial mortgages · Private property

Their pain: TDSR is the wall banks hide behind. Solid property asset, clear exit strategy, but a debt-servicing ratio that blocks bank financing. Or they need a bridging loan to close a transaction before their existing property sells.

"Your property is the asset. Your TDSR isn't the story. Tembusu's TDSR-exempt financing means we evaluate your collateral and commercial case — not just a ratio. Bridging loans and caveat financing from $100K, disbursed fast."
⏱️
Property-Backed Borrowers
Equity unlock · Capital release · Time-sensitive deals · En bloc bridging

Their pain: They own property with real equity — a condo, shophouse, or commercial unit — and they have a time-sensitive reason to deploy it: a business opportunity with a closing window, a gap between purchasing a new property and selling the old one, or a deal where speed is the only thing that matters. Banks move in weeks. The window closes in days.

"Your property is working capital waiting to be deployed. Tembusu turns equity into cash in 24 hours — no TDSR ceiling, no committee delays. Whether you're bridging a transaction, seizing an opportunity, or unlocking capital for your next move, we move at the speed your situation demands."
🚗
Car Buyers & COE Renewers
Used car loans · COE renewal · Corporate vehicle financing · PHV

Their pain: Self-employed, foreign worker, or imperfect credit — bank HP loans come back declined. Or the COE deadline is 10 days away and the bank is quoting three weeks for approval.

"Consistently high loan approval rates. No middlemen. No markup. Tembusu's in-house car loans are TDSR-exempt and available up to 100% for corporate and PHV vehicles — with paperwork fully managed from LTA to ownership transfer."
👤
Individuals & Self-Employed
Personal loans · Self-employed · Foreigners & expats · Urgent financing

Their pain: Variable income, missing payslips, or a credit history that doesn't reflect current reality. Urgent needs — medical, personal, business transition — that can't wait for a bank's approval timeline.

"We take an empathetic approach. Every case is different, and at Tembusu, a human being reviews yours — not an algorithm. Transparent rates, zero hidden fees, and a guided process from application to cash in hand."

Trust / Authority

Property-Backed Borrowers — Option A
Your equity. Unlocked in 24 hours.
Leads with the outcome, not the mechanism. Speaks directly to borrowers who have property and need speed — equity release, bridging, and caveat loan contexts. Pairs with the sub-line: "No TDSR ceiling. No committee delays. Just capital, fast."
Property-Backed Borrowers — Option B
Fast capital for decisions that can't wait.
Urgency-led, but broadly applicable — works across search ads, banners, and page headers without requiring a specific scenario. Speaks to any borrower facing a closing deadline, bridging gap, or time-sensitive opportunity where speed is the deciding factor.

Your property holds the answer. We just move faster.
Asset-empowerment framing — the borrower already has what's needed, Tembusu simply acts on it quickly. Less transactional than Option A, less urgent than Option B. Works well for longer-form placements: landing page heroes, brochure headers, and consultation CTAs.
06 — Activation Guidance

Where & How to Deploy the VP

A value proposition only creates value when it reaches the right person, in the right channel, at the right moment in their decision journey. Below is the activation map across Tembusu's key touchpoints.

Website — Primary Owned Channel

🌐

Homepage Hero

Lead with the master tagline + proof strip. TDSR-exempt and "bank rejected" angles appear in supporting copy — these are qualifying signals, not brand openers.

📄

Service Pages

Each page opens with its segment-specific VP, followed by eligibility expressed as inclusions (who we serve), not exclusions. Close with proof and a CTA.

📝

Priority Content Gaps

Bridging loans (1,900+ monthly searches), bad credit car loans, self-employed loans, and expat financing — each should open with the relevant segment VP narrative.

Outreach & Growth Channels

🔍

Comparison Platform Listings

When pitching SingSaver, MoneySmart, and LoanAdvisor, lead with three hard differentiators: TDSR-exempt products, in-house $100K–$20M range, and 24-hour disbursement.

📞

Inbound Sales Script

Lead with empathy → quick eligibility signal ("We've funded 2,000+ deals, we're TDSR-exempt, businesses from 3 months") → close with urgency ("We can give you a decision today").

📣

Future Paid Channel Path

A marketplace entity or excluded moneylender structure would unlock Google Ads and social media. This VP framework is channel-ready the moment the structure permits.
